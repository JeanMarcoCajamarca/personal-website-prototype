Who is Jean Marco Cajamarca?

Cajamarca was born on October 6, 2002, and raised in the vibrant neighborhood of El Barrio, in the City of New York.

The son of Mexican and Ecuadorian immigrants who worked tirelessly to support their family of seven children, he learned early on the transformative power of education and perseverance. His passion for learning and creative expression began during his school years in NYC, where inspiring teachers and early friendships nurtured his deep love for movies, music, video games, and storytelling.

His high school years at All Hallows High School in the South Bronx sparked a profound appreciation for literature and creative writing. That passion, paired with a desire to express powerful messages through art, initially inspired him to pursue filmmaking. Following the events of 2019–2020, Jean took time to discern his vocation and spent two years at the Redemptoris Mater Seminary and St. John Vianney Theological Seminary in Denver, Colorado, studying philosophy and theology while developing crucial communication and life skills.

Upon returning to NYC, Jean enrolled at CUNY Hunter College, where he is currently a sophomore pursuing a major in English with a concentration in Creative Writing and a minor in Media Studies. Alongside his academic work, he has built practical experience in copy and program development such as crafting frameworks for Sunrize Motivation, a leadership development practice for young adults, and exploring the intersection of creative narrative, web development, and digital media.

Driven by an analytical mindset and a strong spirit of self-reliance, Jean approaches every project with intellectual curiosity and structured creativity. Whether he is writing fiction, reporting for campus media, copyediting literary work, or building custom digital environments, he is committed to using the power of storytelling to connect with others and reflect the world around him.
